How to Measure Digital Marketing ROI: The KPIs That Actually Matter

By Novifaro · Published November 20, 2026 · Updated November 20, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

Digital marketing ROI is (revenue attributed to marketing minus marketing cost) divided by marketing cost. To measure it reliably, track conversions in Google Analytics 4, tag campaigns with UTM parameters, record leads and sales in a CRM or spreadsheet, and review a small set of KPIs monthly: cost per lead, conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, return on ad spend, and customer lifetime value.

If you cannot tell which marketing brings customers, you cannot confidently decide what to keep, fix, or stop. Good measurement does not need an expensive dashboard. It needs consistent tracking and a handful of metrics tied to revenue.

The ROI formula

ROI = (Revenue from marketing − Marketing cost) ÷ Marketing cost. Include ad spend, agency or freelancer fees, software, and the time you invest.

Example (illustrative numbers): if you spend $2,000 in a month and it generates $8,000 in new revenue, ROI is ($8,000 − $2,000) ÷ $2,000 = 3, or 300%.

The KPIs worth tracking

KPIWhat it tells youHow to calculate
Cost per lead (CPL)How efficiently you generate enquiriesMarketing cost ÷ number of leads
Conversion rateHow well visitors become leads or customersConversions ÷ visitors
Customer acquisition cost (CAC)What it costs to win a customerMarketing and sales cost ÷ new customers
Return on ad spend (ROAS)Revenue earned per dollar of ad spendAd revenue ÷ ad spend
Customer lifetime value (LTV)Total value of a customer over timeAverage order value × purchase frequency × customer lifespan
Lead-to-customer rateLead quality and sales follow-upCustomers ÷ leads

A healthy business typically wants customer lifetime value to comfortably exceed acquisition cost. Your target ratio depends on your margins and cash flow.

How to set up tracking

  1. Install Google Analytics 4 and mark key events as conversions: form submissions, calls, bookings, and purchases.
  2. Connect Google Search Console to see organic queries and indexing health.
  3. Use UTM parameters on every campaign link (source, medium, campaign) so traffic is attributed correctly.
  4. Capture the lead source in your form or CRM, and ask new customers how they found you.
  5. Connect leads to revenue. Record which leads became customers and how much they spent.
  6. Build a one-page monthly report with spend, leads, customers, CPL, CAC, and your next actions.

Common measurement mistakes

  • Tracking traffic and followers instead of leads and revenue.
  • Not separating brand-name searches from non-brand searches.
  • Judging channels over days instead of a meaningful period.
  • Ignoring lead quality: cheap leads that never buy are expensive.
  • Forgetting that customers often use several touchpoints before converting, so review assisted conversions as well as last click.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good ROI for digital marketing?

It depends on your margins and business model. A useful benchmark is whether the return on each channel covers its full cost and leaves a profit after delivering the product or service.

What is the difference between ROI and ROAS?

ROAS measures revenue per dollar of ad spend. ROI measures profit relative to total marketing cost, which can include fees, software, and time.

How do I track phone calls and in-person sales?

Use call tracking numbers, dedicated booking links, promo codes, and a "How did you hear about us?" question to connect offline results to marketing.

How often should I review marketing performance?

Check leading indicators weekly, review full results monthly, and make strategy changes quarterly so you give campaigns enough time to work.
