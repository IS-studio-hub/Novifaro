What Is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)? How to Get Your Business Cited by AI

By Novifaro · Published November 20, 2026 · Updated November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring your website and content so AI search tools such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Claude can find, understand, and cite your business in their answers. It builds on SEO fundamentals and adds answer-first writing, consistent business information, structured data, and credible original content.

More people now ask an AI assistant a question instead of scrolling a page of blue links. If an AI tool answers "who is the best web design agency in Toronto?" or "how do I improve my website's conversion rate?", it is choosing a handful of sources to read, summarize, and sometimes cite. GEO is how you earn a place in those answers.

GEO vs SEO: what is the difference?

SEO (search engine optimization) helps your pages rank in a list of results. GEO helps AI systems retrieve, trust, and quote your content inside a generated answer. They overlap heavily: a page that is crawlable, fast, clear, and authoritative is easier for both a search engine and an AI model to use.

How AI tools decide what to cite

AI search products generally combine a language model with retrieval: they fetch relevant pages from a search index or their own crawl, then write an answer using those pages. Nobody outside these companies can promise how a specific tool ranks sources, and any agency that guarantees AI citations is overselling. What you can control is how easy and safe your content is to use.

  • Accessible: crawlers can fetch the page, and the main content is in the HTML, not hidden behind scripts or logins.
  • Answer-first: the page states the answer in the first sentences, then supports it.
  • Specific: names, numbers, steps, locations, and definitions beat vague marketing language.
  • Consistent: your business name, services, and location match across your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, and directories.
  • Credible: original examples, case studies, and clear authorship signal that the content is trustworthy.

The 8-step GEO checklist

  1. Let the right crawlers in. Check robots.txt and your firewall. If you want AI visibility, do not block search-focused crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and Googlebot. Training-focused crawlers (for example GPTBot and Google-Extended) are a separate business decision.
  2. Lead with the answer. Start every page and every section with a one to three sentence direct answer. This is the passage AI tools are most likely to lift.
  3. Use question-style headings. Headings such as "How much does SEO cost?" mirror how people prompt AI tools.
  4. Add structured data. Use schema.org markup (Organization, LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService, Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList) so machines can read facts without guessing.
  5. Keep your entity consistent. Use the same business name, description, address, phone, and service names everywhere.
  6. Publish original value. Share your own process, benchmarks you actually measured, and real project outcomes. Generic rewrites of other articles give AI no reason to cite you.
  7. Show freshness and authorship. Put clear publish and update dates and a named author or organization on every article.
  8. Make the site fast and simple. Server-rendered HTML, good Core Web Vitals, clean headings, and an XML sitemap help every crawler. An llms.txt file is a low-cost optional extra, but support varies, so treat it as a bonus.

What does not work

  • Keyword stuffing or hidden text. Both search engines and AI systems discount it.
  • Mass-produced AI filler with no original information.
  • Fake reviews or invented statistics. They damage trust and can break platform rules.
  • Promises of guaranteed rankings or guaranteed AI mentions.

How to measure GEO

GEO measurement is still maturing, so combine several signals instead of relying on one number:

  • In Google Analytics 4, review referral traffic from sources such as chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, and copilot.microsoft.com.
  • Run a monthly set of 10 to 20 real customer questions in major AI tools and record whether your brand is mentioned or cited.
  • Track branded search growth and impressions in Google Search Console.
  • Watch the metric that pays the bills: leads and sales from visitors who arrive from AI tools.

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO in simple terms?

GEO is optimizing your content so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity can understand it and mention or cite your business when they answer questions.

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO. Google AI Overviews draw on pages that Google Search already indexes, and other AI tools rely on search indexes and crawls too, so strong SEO remains the foundation.

How do I get my business to show up in ChatGPT?

Make your site crawlable, publish clear answer-first pages about your services and location, keep your business details consistent across the web, add structured data, and earn credible mentions. There is no guarantee, but these steps make your business easier to find and cite.

Does llms.txt help?

It is a proposed convention that gives AI tools a plain-text summary of your site. It is cheap to add, but major platforms have not confirmed they depend on it, so do not expect it to replace good content and SEO.

How long does GEO take to work?

Like SEO, results usually take months, not days. Fresh, specific, well-structured content can be picked up faster than thin pages, but timing depends on your competition and how often AI tools refresh their sources.
