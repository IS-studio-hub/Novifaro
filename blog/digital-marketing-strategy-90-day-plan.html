Digital Marketing Strategy for Small Business: A 90-Day Plan

By Novifaro · Published November 20, 2026 · Updated November 20, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

A strong small business digital marketing plan has three phases: in days 1 to 30 set goals, fix tracking, and prepare your website and profiles; in days 31 to 60 launch SEO content, email, and one paid channel test; in days 61 to 90 analyze results, double down on what works, and improve conversion. Focus on fewer channels done well.

Most small businesses do not fail at marketing because of a lack of effort; they spread effort across too many channels with no tracking. A 90-day plan gives you focus, a clear review point, and a way to prove what drives leads and sales.

Days 1 to 30: foundation

  1. Set one measurable goal. For example: 30 qualified leads per month or a target number of booked consultations.
  2. Define your ideal customer. Who they are, what problem they have, what they search for, and what makes them choose you.
  3. Fix tracking. Set up Google Analytics 4 key events, Google Search Console, UTM links, and a simple lead log.
  4. Prepare your website. Clear messaging, a visible call to action, fast mobile pages, and service pages for what you want to sell.
  5. Claim and complete your profiles. Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, and relevant directories with consistent details.

Days 31 to 60: launch

  • SEO and content: publish 4 to 8 helpful pages or articles aimed at real customer questions and link them to your service pages.
  • Email: add a signup offer, send a welcome message, and share useful updates regularly.
  • One paid channel test: choose Google Ads for high-intent searches, or Meta ads for visual products and local offers. Start with a modest, defined budget.
  • Social proof: collect reviews and testimonials and display them on key pages.

Days 61 to 90: measure and scale

  • Compare each channel by cost per lead and lead quality, not only volume.
  • Increase investment in the best performer and pause or fix the weakest.
  • Improve landing pages using what you learned: headlines, forms, and calls to action.
  • Consider an AI assistant or automated follow-up to respond to leads faster.
  • Plan the next 90 days from the data.

Frequently asked questions

How much should a small business spend on digital marketing?

There is no single right number. Base it on your profit margins and growth goal, begin with a test budget, and scale channels where the cost of acquiring a customer is lower than the customer's value.

Should I start with SEO or paid ads?

Ads can bring leads quickly while SEO compounds over time. Many businesses use both: ads for speed and learning, SEO for sustainable growth.

How long before digital marketing shows results?

Paid campaigns can produce leads within days, while SEO and content often take a few months. Review progress at the 90-day mark.

Do I need a digital marketing agency?

An agency helps when you lack time or expertise, need integrated SEO, web, and AI support, or want to move faster. Make sure they report on leads and revenue, not just traffic.
