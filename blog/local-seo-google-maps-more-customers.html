Local SEO: How to Get More Customers from Google Maps and "Near Me" Searches

By Novifaro · Published November 26, 2026 · Updated November 26, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

Local SEO brings more customers by making your business appear in Google Maps and the local results when people search for what you offer nearby. The three levers Google names are relevance, distance, and prominence. You influence them by completing your Google Business Profile, keeping your business details consistent everywhere, earning and answering reviews, and publishing clear service pages for the areas you genuinely serve.

When someone searches "plumber near me" or "web design agency Toronto", they are usually ready to call, book, or visit. Showing up in that moment is one of the most direct ways a business can bring in new customers. At Novifaro we treat local SEO as a customer acquisition system: it connects your Google Business Profile, your website, your reviews, and your tracking so every search becomes a measurable lead.

How Google decides who shows up locally

Google says local results are based mainly on three factors: relevance (how well your profile matches the search), distance (how close you are to the searcher or the location in the query), and prominence (how well known and trusted your business is, based on reviews, links, and information across the web). You cannot change your distance from a customer, but you can improve the other two.

FactorWhat it meansWhat you can do
RelevanceYour profile and site match what the person searched forChoose accurate categories, list services, and write clear service pages
DistanceHow near your business is to the searcherSet your service area honestly and keep your address accurate
ProminenceHow established and trusted you appearEarn reviews, get cited in directories, publish helpful content

The Novifaro local SEO system in 6 steps

  1. Optimize your Google Business Profile. Claim and verify it, pick the best primary category, add every service you offer, set accurate hours, and upload real photos.
  2. Make your business information consistent. Your name, address, and phone number should match on your website, your profile, social pages, and directories.
  3. Build service pages that match local intent. Create a focused page for each core service, written for the questions customers actually ask, with your location, proof, and a clear call to action.
  4. Add location pages only where they are real. A page for a neighbourhood or city you genuinely serve can help. Copying the same text and swapping the city name does not.
  5. Earn and answer reviews. Ask happy customers at the right moment, make it easy with a direct link, and reply professionally to every review.
  6. Track calls, directions, and leads. Use Google Business Profile insights, call tracking, and Google Analytics 4 events so you know which searches produce customers.

Win "near me" and AI-assisted local searches

People increasingly ask AI tools local questions too, such as "who is a reliable accountant in Etobicoke?" These tools lean on the same public signals: a clear business description, consistent details, reviews, and pages that answer the question directly. Put a short, factual summary of what you do and where you do it near the top of your key pages, and add LocalBusiness schema markup so machines can read your details without guessing.

Common local SEO mistakes

  • Using a virtual office or PO box as your address, which can get your profile suspended.
  • Keyword-stuffing your business name. Use your real name.
  • Ignoring negative reviews instead of responding helpfully.
  • Letting hours, phone numbers, or services go out of date.
  • Buying fake reviews. It breaks platform rules and destroys trust.

Frequently asked questions

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the practice of improving your visibility in location-based searches, including Google Maps and the local results, so nearby customers can find, call, and visit your business.

How long does local SEO take to work?

Profile and review improvements can shift results within weeks, while stronger rankings in competitive areas often take a few months of consistent work.

Do I need a website for local SEO?

A Google Business Profile helps on its own, but a fast website with clear service pages gives Google and AI tools more information to trust, and gives customers a place to convert.

How many reviews do I need?

There is no magic number. Aim for a steady flow of genuine, recent reviews that mention your services, and compare yourself with the businesses ranking above you.
