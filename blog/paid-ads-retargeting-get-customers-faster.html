Google Ads and Retargeting: How Paid Marketing Brings Customers Faster While SEO Builds

By Novifaro · Published November 26, 2026 · Updated November 26, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

Paid ads bring customers faster than SEO because you can appear in front of people ready to buy as soon as a campaign is approved. Use Google Ads to capture people who are actively searching, Meta ads to create demand with visual and local offers, and retargeting to bring back visitors who did not convert. Always send traffic to a focused landing page and track conversions, so you scale only what pays back.

SEO compounds over months. Paid advertising works in days. The strongest growth plans use both: ads to generate customers and data now, SEO to lower your cost per customer over time. Here is how Novifaro approaches paid marketing so every dollar is tied to leads and revenue.

The 6-step paid growth process

  1. Set the goal and the numbers. Decide your target cost per lead or cost per customer based on your margins.
  2. Build a focused landing page. One offer, one call to action, fast on mobile, with proof. Sending ads to a generic homepage wastes budget.
  3. Install conversion tracking first. Track form submissions, calls, and bookings in Google Analytics 4 and in the ad platforms before spending.
  4. Start narrow and test. Launch with a small set of keywords or audiences and a few ad variations. Review search terms and exclude irrelevant ones.
  5. Add retargeting. Show helpful follow-up messages to visitors who read your service pages or started a form, with a frequency cap so you do not annoy people.
  6. Optimize weekly, scale monthly. Pause what does not convert, increase budget on what pays back, and keep improving the landing page.

How paid ads and SEO work together

  • Test with ads, win with SEO. Use paid search data to see which keywords and messages convert, then build SEO content around the winners.
  • Cover more of the results page. Appearing in both ads and organic results can increase your visibility for important searches.
  • Share the data. Insights from ad copy and landing page tests improve your website for every visitor.

Rules and responsible tracking in Canada

Be transparent about tracking and cookies, follow each ad platform's policies, and respect privacy law such as PIPEDA. If you email leads, comply with CASL by getting consent and including an unsubscribe option. Seek legal advice for your specific situation.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly do paid ads bring customers?

Campaigns can start producing clicks and leads within days of launching, though the first weeks are usually about learning which keywords, audiences, and messages work best.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

Work backwards from your goal: if a customer is worth a certain profit and you convert a known share of leads, you can calculate what you can afford to pay per lead. Start with a test budget and scale what is profitable.

What is retargeting?

Retargeting, also called remarketing, shows ads to people who already visited your website or engaged with your brand, reminding them to come back and finish what they started.

Should I choose paid ads or SEO?

For most businesses the answer is both. Paid ads give speed and data, SEO builds a lasting, lower-cost source of customers.
